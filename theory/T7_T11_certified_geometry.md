## T7 — Second-order external-mode correction

Partition the modal coordinates into the selected boundary block `B={2,3}` and external modes `O={1,4}`.

A near-identity block-diagonalizing expansion gives an effective boundary block

`M_eff = Lambda_B + epsilon E_BB + epsilon^2 Omega_B + O(epsilon^3)`.

For `i,j in B`,

`Omega_ij = (1/2) sum_{k in O} E_ik E_kj [1/(lambda_i-lambda_k)+1/(lambda_j-lambda_k)]`.

The resulting second-order discriminant is

`Disc_eff = [g+epsilon X+epsilon^2 U]^2 + 4[epsilon Y+epsilon^2 V][epsilon Z+epsilon^2 W]`.

The leading external correction is the self-energy imbalance `U=Omega_22-Omega_33`.

Static algebraic verification confirmed that the reduced boundary eigenvalues had approximately `O(epsilon^3)` error, with median log-log exponent about `3.01` in random 4D tests.
